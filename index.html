About me

I'm a master's student in Applied Computing at UFABC (Brazil), researching robotics at the IARA++ lab: odometry, digital twins and multi-robot systems with ROS 2. I also work as a lab technician at UFABC. I teach robotics to beginners as a volunteer at IEEE RAS UFABC (RobotIEEE project, 1st place at the IEEE Brazil Council SAC Awards 2024) and teach the Electronics module at Outcome School. Before my master's, I worked in R&D consulting and at Itaú bank, and did an academic exchange at the Shibaura Institute of Technology, Japan.

Projects

Digital Twin of a Tracked Robot (ROS 2)

Problem
Low-cost robots have poor odometry, which makes it hard to trust a digital twin of them.
What I did
Built a digital twin for a low-cost tracked robot, with off-MCU odometry validated by an overhead ArUco camera.
  • ROS 2
  • Python

ArUco Ground Truth Camera System

Problem
Measuring where robots really are, to validate their odometry.
What I did
Built an embedded camera on a Raspberry Pi that tracks robots in real time with ArUco markers, integrated as a ROS 2 node.
  • Raspberry Pi
  • OpenCV
  • ArUco
  • ROS 2
