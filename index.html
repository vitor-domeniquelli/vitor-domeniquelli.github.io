About me

I'm a master's student in Applied Computing at UFABC (Brazil), researching robotics at the IARA++ lab: odometry, digital twins and multi-robot systems with ROS 2. I also work as a lab technician at UFABC. I teach robotics to beginners as a volunteer at IEEE RAS UFABC (RobotIEEE project, 1st place at the IEEE Brazil Council SAC Awards 2024) and teach the Electronics module at Outcome School. Before my master's, I worked in R&D consulting and at Itaú bank.
